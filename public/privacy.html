5. Account & Data Deletion Policy ("Right to Erasure")

In accordance with Article 17 of the UK GDPR ("Right to be Forgotten") and mobile store guidelines, all clients and vendors have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

5.1 How to Delete Your Account

  1. In-App Self-Service (Recommended): Log in to the EWA app, navigate to Profile → Delete Account, review the warning, type DELETE into the confirmation field, and tap Delete My Account. Your session is terminated immediately and account data is queued for permanent erasure.
  2. Online Web Request: Submit an account deletion request online at ewaofficialapp.com/data-deletion.html.
  3. Email Request: Send an email from your registered email address to support@ewaofficialapp.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

5.2 The Deletion Lifecycle & Timeline

  • Immediate Action (Within 24 Hours): Your account is deactivated. Vendor service catalogs, portfolio pictures, and client profiles are immediately delisted from search. Authentication tokens and notification endpoints are revoked.
  • Statutory Erasure Window (Within 30 Days): In compliance with UK GDPR Article 12(3), personal credentials, contact info, device tokens, and stored media are permanently erased from our production databases and storage buckets.
  • Statutory Accounting Anonymisation: To satisfy HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) statutory record-keeping rules (UK GDPR Art. 17(3)(b)), historical booking receipts and payment ledger entries are retained in an irreversibly anonymised format with personal names, contact info, and user references permanently severed and set to null.
