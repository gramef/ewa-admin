Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

EWA — Afro Hair & Beauty Services Platform

EWA is dedicated to protecting your privacy and providing transparent control over your personal data. In compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), Google Play Data Safety policies, and Apple App Store Review Guidelines (5.1.1(v)), this page describes how our users and vendors can request the deletion of their account and data, what data is permanently erased, and what statutory retention periods apply.

Self-Service In-App Deletion
You can permanently delete your EWA account directly within the mobile application at any time without needing to contact customer support.

1. Step-by-Step Instructions to Delete Your Account

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the EWA Client or EWA Vendor app on your device.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Tap the Profile icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen and tap Delete Account (marked in red).
  5. Read the warning prompt, which explains that this action is permanent and irreversible.
  6. Type the word DELETE into the confirmation field to unlock the delete button.
  7. Tap Delete My Account. Your session is terminated immediately, and automated data erasure begins.

Method 2: Email Request to Data Protection Officer

If you have uninstalled the app or are unable to access your device, you may submit an account deletion request via email:

  • Send an email to: support@ewaofficial.co.uk
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Include: Your registered full name, account email address, and phone number.

Our Data Protection Team will verify your identity and process your request within 30 calendar days as required by UK GDPR Article 12(3), providing written confirmation upon completion.

2. Data Retention Schedule

EWA retains personal and business data strictly according to statutory retention schedules and data minimisation principles:

Data Category Retention Period Legal & Operational Basis
Account Profile (Name, Email, Phone, Bio) Active duration + 30 days after deletion request Contractual necessity; permanently scrubbed upon deletion.
Inactive / Dormant Accounts 24 months inactivity Automated warning sent at 23 months; permanently pruned if unconfirmed.
Financial & Invoicing Records (Bookings, Payouts) 6 years following the end of the financial tax year UK HMRC statutory requirement (Section 12B Taxes Management Act 1970 for business and tax audit). Retained in irreversibly anonymised format (`user_id = null`).
Vendor Identity Verification (Persona KYC) 5 years post-account closure UK Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations; facial biometric check data is purged within 30 days of check completion.
In-App Chat Messages 12 months rolling Dispute resolution and trust & safety auditing; automatically deleted thereafter.
Real-Time Location Data Duration of active dispatch Real-time provider routing only; historical booking logs store only static address.
Server Logs & Security Records 90 days to 12 months Platform cyber-defense, rate limiting, and fraud prevention.

3. What Happens Upon Account Deletion

  • Immediate Effect (Within 24 Hours): Your account is deactivated. Vendor service menus, portfolio photographs, and customer profile cards are immediately unlisted from all search discovery. All active authentication tokens and push notification subscriptions are invalidated.
  • Permanent Erasure (Within 30 Days): Your name, email, phone number, password hash, stored addresses, uploaded portfolio media, and favorite lists are permanently wiped from our production databases and storage buckets.
  • Statutory Accounting Anonymisation: As permitted under UK GDPR Article 17(3)(b) & (e) to fulfill HMRC legal tax audit requirements, historical booking transaction amounts and dates are retained in an irreversibly anonymised format (personal identifiers are stripped and permanently unlinked).
